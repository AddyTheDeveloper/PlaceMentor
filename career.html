    Resume Building (CO4)

    Rule of One: One page, one clear objective, one cohesive font family (preferably Inter, Outfit, or Helvetica).

    Structure your experience bullet points using the STAR Method:

    S

    Situation

    Describe the context or problem.

    T

    Task

    Identify the goal or challenges.

    A

    Action

    Detail the actions you took.

    R

    Result

    Highlight quantified outcomes.

    Pro-Tip: Always use metrics! "Optimized sorting execution time by 40%" is vastly better than saying "Wrote a sorting algorithm."

    Behavioral Interview Scenarios (CO6)

    "Tell me about a time you failed."
    Focus strictly on the learning, adaptability, and subsequent recovery, not the failure itself.

    ScenarioBest Approach
    Conflict in TeamExplain how you mediated using objective data and neutral, empathetic communication.
    Tight DeadlineShowcase prioritization, scope management, and time tracking (Agile/Scrum mindset).
    Strength / WeaknessWeakness must be real but active/actionable (e.g., "I sometimes over-focus on details, so I started using timers").
    Why should we hire you?Align your unique skillsets and projects with the company's current challenges or needs.
    Where do you see yourself in 5 years?Focus on growth, technical skill mastery, and contributing to the organization's goals.
